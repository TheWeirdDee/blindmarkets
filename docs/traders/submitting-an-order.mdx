title: Submitting an Order
description: How to place an encrypted trade on BlindMarkets, from wallet connection to settlement
---

<Note>
  BlindMarkets is on **Starknet Sepolia testnet**. You'll need STRK for gas — get it free at the [Starknet faucet](https://starknet-faucet.vercel.app).
</Note>

## Before you start

You need:
- Argent X or Braavos wallet, connected to Sepolia
- STRK for gas fees
- A whitelisted token to swap (STRK, WBTC, or USDC on Sepolia)

---

## Connect your wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** in the top right of the app. Argent X and Braavos are both supported. Nothing happens on-chain until you submit an order — connecting is read-only.

---

## Build your order

Open the **Dashboard** and fill in the Intent Composer on the left:

**Sell** — the token you're giving up and how much.

**Receive** — the token you want back and the minimum amount you'll accept. If solvers can't meet this minimum, your order won't fill.

**Max fee** — the most you're willing to pay the solver, in basis points. `50` = 0.5%. Solvers compete to charge you less.

**Deadline** — how many minutes until the order expires if it hasn't been filled. After expiry, nothing moves.

**Privacy mode** — controls what solvers can see before the batch closes. See [Privacy Modes](/traders/privacy-modes) for the trade-offs.

---

## Submit and approve

Click **Submit Order**. Two things happen in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gateway receives the encrypted order">
    Your browser encrypts the order payload and sends it to the gateway. The gateway stores an encrypted blob — it cannot read your amounts until the batch window closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your wallet signs the on-chain commitment">
    A wallet prompt appears asking you to sign a transaction to the IntentRegistry contract on Starknet. This commits a Pedersen hash of your order — not the order itself — on-chain. Approve it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You must approve the wallet transaction for your order to be valid. If you close the wallet prompt, the gateway still has your encrypted order but there's no on-chain commitment — the order will be rejected when the batch closes.
</Warning>

---

## Wait for settlement

Once the wallet transaction confirms, your order is in the current batch window. You don't need to do anything else.

The batch closes within 30 seconds. Solvers then compete to fill it. A typical round-trip from submission to settlement is under 90 seconds.

---

## Order status lifecycle

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Submitted to gateway, wallet tx not yet confirmed |
| **Committed** | Wallet tx confirmed on Starknet |
| **In Auction** | Batch is closed, solvers competing |
| **Settled** | Filled — tokens are in your wallet |
| **Failed** | Batch failed — order requeued for next window |
| **Expired** | Deadline passed before settlement |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled before the auction started |

---

## Verifying your settlement

Every settled order is a Starknet transaction you can inspect on [Starkscan](https://sepolia.starkscan.co). Look up your wallet address to see the full history of commitments and settlements.
