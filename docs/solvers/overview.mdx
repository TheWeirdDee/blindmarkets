## What is a solver?

A solver is any participant who fills batches of trader orders. When a batch window closes, solvers receive the decrypted orders and race to find the best way to fill them. The solver with the best outcome for traders — most output, lowest fees — wins the auction and executes the settlement.

Solvers are the mechanism that turns encrypted orders into real token transfers. Without solvers, no orders settle.

---

## How the auction works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Batch closes">
    Every 30 seconds the coordinator closes the current batch window and announces the batch on-chain via the BatchAuction contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Solvers receive decrypted orders">
    After the batch is announced, the gateway decrypts the orders and makes them available to registered solvers via the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Solvers compute solutions">
    Each solver independently calculates the best way to fill all orders in the batch — routing through DEXes, filling from inventory, or matching orders against each other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Solvers submit solutions">
    Solvers call the BatchAuction contract with their proposed solution. The contract scores each submission based on: trader output amounts, solver fee, and solver track record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Winner settles">
    The solver with the highest score wins. They must then call BatchSettlement to execute the actual token transfers. If they fail to settle within the deadline, they are slashed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Scoring

The BatchAuction contract scores solutions on three factors:

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Trader output** | How much each trader receives vs their minimum. Higher is better. |
| **Solver fee** | The fee claimed from traders. Lower is better for traders. |
| **Track record** | Historical settlement success rate. Good solvers get a small bonus. |

The exact scoring formula is in `contracts/src/batch_auction.cairo`.

---

## What solvers earn

Solvers earn the difference between the prices they route through and the prices traders locked in, plus any explicit fee (capped at the `max_fee_bps` each trader set).

There is no guaranteed fee — solvers compete, and the winner is the one offering the best deal for traders.

---

## Requirements

To participate as a solver:

1. **Post a bond** — deposit STRK into the SolverBond contract. Minimum is 1 STRK on Sepolia.
2. **Register** — your solver account must be active (bond deposited).
3. **Access the API** — you'll need a gateway API key to fetch decrypted orders.
