## Why bonds exist

Bonds align solver incentives with trader outcomes. A solver who wins an auction but fails to settle loses part of their bond. This makes it unprofitable to bid on batches you can't fill.

---

## Depositing a bond

Call `deposit` on the SolverBond contract with the amount of STRK you want to bond. You must approve the STRK transfer first.

**SolverBond address:** `0x069db55d725a0a4f48705ebecf0993e455e2b3716940081005b48d779e7301e8`

Using `sncast`:

```bash
# 1. Approve STRK spend
sncast --account your_account invoke \
  --network sepolia \
  --contract-address 0x04718f5a0fc34cc1af16a1cdee98ffb20c31f5cd61d6ab07201858f4287c938d \
  --function approve \
  --calldata 0x069db55d725a0a4f48705ebecf0993e455e2b3716940081005b48d779e7301e8 1000000000000000000 0

# 2. Deposit into SolverBond (1 STRK = 10^18)
sncast --account your_account invoke \
  --network sepolia \
  --contract-address 0x069db55d725a0a4f48705ebecf0993e455e2b3716940081005b48d779e7301e8 \
  --function deposit \
  --calldata 1000000000000000000 0
```

**Minimum bond:** 1 STRK on Sepolia testnet.

```bash
sncast --account your_account call \
  --network sepolia \
  --contract-address 0x069db55d725a0a4f48705ebecf0993e455e2b3716940081005b48d779e7301e8 \
  --function get_bond \
  --calldata YOUR_SOLVER_ADDRESS
```

## Slashing

If a solver wins a batch auction but fails to call `BatchSettlement.execute` within the settlement deadline, they are slashed:

- A percentage of their bond is deducted and transferred to a protocol treasury
- Repeated failures can result in blacklisting from future auctions

The slash percentage is configured at the protocol level (currently 10% of bond per failed settlement on Sepolia).

<Warning>
  If your solver process crashes after winning an auction, you must settle before the deadline or face a slash. Monitor your solver process closely and have a recovery path.
</Warning>

## Withdrawing a bond

You can withdraw your bond when you're not currently obligated to settle a batch:

```bash
sncast --account your_account invoke \
  --network sepolia \
  --contract-address 0x069db55d725a0a4f48705ebecf0993e455e2b3716940081005b48d779e7301e8 \
  --function withdraw \
  --calldata AMOUNT_FELT 0
```

There is a cooldown period after your last batch win before you can withdraw, to ensure you can't front-run the slashing mechanism.
