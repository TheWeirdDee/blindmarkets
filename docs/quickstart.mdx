<Note>
  BlindMarkets is currently live on **Starknet Sepolia testnet**. You'll need a funded Sepolia wallet to follow this guide. Mainnet is coming after the audit.
</Note>

## What you'll need

- [Argent X](https://www.argent.xyz/argent-x/) or [Braavos](https://braavos.app/) wallet installed in your browser
- STRK on Sepolia for gas — get it free from the [Starknet faucet](https://starknet-faucet.vercel.app)
- Any whitelisted testnet token (WBTC, USDC, STRK) to swap

---

## Step 1 — Connect your wallet

Open [blindmarkets.xyz](https://blindmarkets.xyz) and click **Connect Wallet** in the top right corner.

Select your wallet (Argent X or Braavos). Your address will appear in the navigation bar once connected.

<Tip>
  If you see "Wrong network", open your wallet settings and switch to **Starknet Sepolia**. The chain ID is `0x534e5f5345504f4c4941`.
</Tip>

---

## Step 2 — Open the Intent Composer

Go to the **Dashboard**. You'll see the order form on the left side of the screen.

Fill in your order:
- **Sell** — which token you're giving up and how much
- **Receive** — which token you want and the minimum you'll accept
- **Max fee** — the maximum solver fee you'll pay (in basis points; 50 = 0.5%)
- **Deadline** — how long the order stays open if not filled (in minutes)
- **Privacy** — how much solvers can see before the batch closes

---

## Step 3 — Submit and sign

Click **Submit Order**. Two things happen:

1. Your browser encrypts the order payload and sends it to the gateway
2. Your wallet prompts you to sign an on-chain commitment transaction on Starknet

Approve the wallet transaction. Once it confirms, your order is live and will be included in the next batch window.

<Note>
  The on-chain transaction commits a **hash** of your order, not the order itself. The actual amounts stay encrypted until the batch closes.
</Note>

---

## Step 4 — Watch for settlement

The dashboard shows your order's status in real time. A normal fill takes under a minute:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pending** | Submitted — wallet tx not yet confirmed |
| **Committed** | Wallet tx confirmed on Starknet |
| **In Auction** | Batch closed — solvers competing |
| **Settled** | Filled — tokens are in your wallet |

---

## Step 5 — Verify on Voyager

Every settlement is a public Starknet transaction. Visit [sepolia.voyager.online](https://sepolia.voyager.online) and look up your wallet address to see your full order history.
